## Course contact and submissions

For course questions, personal matters and assessed submissions, email [submit.progmis@gmail.com](mailto:submit.progmis@gmail.com). Keep complete assessed work and personal information out of shared GitHub Issues. Exact deadlines and file-naming instructions are announced separately.

## Weekly reflection and feedback

[Open the weekly reflection form](https://forms.gle/Z3JhR3GHmHDWKxuN7)

After class, choose the correct teaching week and submit a new response. Use the same form each week.

Responses are not anonymous. Your participation report supports instructor review; submitting it does not automatically prove attendance, and a missing response needs follow-up rather than an automatic absence decision.
