## What the midterm checks

This midterm checks your **understanding** of Weeks 1–4 in a fresh everyday setting (not memorising syntax):

| Part | Task | Suggested time | Points |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Read & trace code by hand (types, loop trace, records) | 12 min | 24 |
| 2 | Turn a flowchart into a rule function + explain a policy change | 15 min | 24 |
| 3 | Work with a list of records and return a summary dictionary | 18 min | 28 |
| 4 | Audit, test & repair AI-written code + harm report | 15 min | 24 |
| End | **My key line** (mandatory ownership check) + clean run | 1 min | required |

**Parts are independent.** If you get stuck on one part, move on and come back later.
