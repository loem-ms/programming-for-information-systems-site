# Lecturer: Mengsay Loem

Mengsay Loem is a **part-time lecturer at National University of Battambang, Cambodia (2026–present)**, teaching this Programming for Information Systems course. He is also an **engineer at Preferred Networks, Inc., Japan (July 2026–present)**, where he works on building **large language models (LLMs)**.

Previously, he was an **R&D researcher at Sansan, Inc., Japan (April 2024–June 2026)**, working on **AI for document understanding and information extraction**.

He earned an **M.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence** and a **B.Eng. in Computer Science** at Tokyo Institute of Technology in Japan, after studying Information Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Kagoshima College. He has also contributed to the TokyoTech-LLM project and served as IT Committee Chair of the Cambodian Students’ Association in Japan. [Education and experience](https://loem-ms.github.io/#education)

**Outside work, he enjoys hiking, walking, sauna and hot springs.**

In this course, students practice a habit that matters in both software engineering and research: make a prediction, run a small example, check the evidence, and explain what it means. You do not need an AI background to begin.

For questions that could help classmates, use the course repository’s **Issues** during or after class once the course repository is available. Include the week, a small input, the expected result and what happened. Keep personal matters, attendance explanations and private assessed work out of Issues; email [submit.progmis@gmail.com](mailto:submit.progmis@gmail.com) for private submissions and personal matters. Issues are visible to everyone who can access the repository; they are not private messages to the instructor.

Read the [course guide](COURSE_GUIDE.md), [setup guide](SETUP.md) and [course links](COURSE_LINKS.md) to get started.
